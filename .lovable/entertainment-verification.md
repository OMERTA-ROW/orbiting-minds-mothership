# Entertainment Galaxy — private first phase

## Implemented
- `/entertainment` is reachable from global navigation/command search, OmniBridge and Command Nexus. Reuses existing Parallel World IDs without changing saved Nexus drafts; Cyberace stays an inert example. No separate project accessed.
- Six selectable studios: Film/Postproduction, Music/Sound, AI Video, Camera/Capture, Virtual Production/3D and Media Code Crucible.
- Three authored demo templates with generated concept artwork, rights/credit declarations and explicit fictional provenance. No media ingest or upload enabled.
- Scene-to-Screen produces three selected-studio templates, retaining the natural-language goal as the brief. It is deterministic, not model-generated or semantic goal interpretation. Processes have editable titles, beats, durations, explicit roles and proposal/review/skip status; scopes, affected sample paths and proposed checks stay fixed.
- Required local storage consent. Separate validated browser-local records, project-scoped views/exports, local history and immutable prior planning checkpoints. Edits/restores create revisions and invalidate old local review. Neither checkpoint nor local restore represents a processed asset or file backup.
- Human declarations require every process reviewed/skipped, all scope previews, rights and privacy checks. Third-party publication intent requires its own confirmation. Approval never enables publishing or execution. Withdrawal is possible during pause and with unreadable policy records.
- Searchable studio/status-filtered authored adapter matrix. Every outside integration is NOT CONNECTED; local planner is DEMO. No VERIFIED or AVAILABLE AFTER AUTH claim. Connectivity timestamps and signatures remain null, no probes, rates unconfigured. Disabled manifest exports and original Unity/Blender inert references.
- A/B plays original bounded quiet synthetic PCM tones only after click; switching pauses playback. Theoretical sine peak/RMS numbers explicitly are not LUFS or uploaded-audio measurements. No microphone/OS API.
- Crucible compares supplied bounded numeric values: 30 s × 24 fps = 720 frames, candidate delta and declared full-scale risk. No decoder, renderer, arbitrary code, independent test or artifact hashes.
