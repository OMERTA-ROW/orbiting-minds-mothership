Existing project binding required, strict arguments, fixed metadata only. No case contents read, logged or uploaded, no command writes, no model/network/credentials. CLI/browser state separate.

Draft edits and confirmation revocation invalidate packets; late preparation results/errors are revision guarded. All caption/content changes clear privacy review. PDF generation rejects more than 1,200 total wrapped lines, bounding newline-heavy inputs.
